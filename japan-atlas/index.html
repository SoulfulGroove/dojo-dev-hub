How to read this atlas

Evidence grades & missing data

A: exact original passage corroborated (none assigned). B: supported by reviewed scholarly or institutional access point. C: broad-period evidence, approximate claim or open hypothesis.

No resource or trade activity is marked “absent” merely because a source does not mention it. The 1585 / 1600 / 1615 / 1645 research snapshots remain unasserted until individually sourced.

The terrain and modern locators are orientation aids; historical coastlines, provinces and boundaries have not been georeferenced.
